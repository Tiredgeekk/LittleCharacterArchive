ENTRY NO. 001 · A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

So, what is this place?

This is a little home for all the characters I've created and collected along the way.

Some have entire worlds built around them. Some have complicated histories, friendships, rivalries, and secrets. Others are still little ideas waiting to become something more.

Rather than letting all those details disappear into scattered documents and forgotten notes, I wanted somewhere to keep them together.

And so, this little library was born. ♡

ENTRY NO. 002 · THE KEEPER

A note about the keeper

Hello! I'm the person behind this little archive.

I love creating characters, building stories, exploring fictional worlds, and finding all the little details that make someone feel real.

This archive is a personal creative space. It's a place to experiment, keep track of ideas, and give my characters somewhere to exist outside my imagination.

Current interests:

  • Character creation
  • Worldbuilding
  • Dungeons & Dragons
  • Stories and lore
  • Making things unnecessarily decorative
ENTRY NO. 003 · THE COLLECTION

What's on the shelves?

The library contains a collection of original characters from different stories, settings, and creative projects.

You'll find things like:

  • Character profiles and reference sheets
  • Personality notes and little quirks
  • Backstories, histories, and lore
  • Relationships and important connections
  • Physical details and character statistics
  • Extra scraps, artwork, and miscellaneous notes

Some entries might be wonderfully complete. Others might still be a work in progress. That's part of the fun!
